## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an artisanal, high-end European gourmet editorial aesthetic celebrating home gastronomy, traditional culinary craftsmanship, and holiday warmth. Created for a prestigious culinary contest partnership between an established home cooking portal and heritage European craft manufacturers (Italian precision engineering and French ceramic cookware), the experience blends rusticity with refined editorial luxury.

The style fuses **Warm Minimalism** with **Tactile European Editorial Craft**:
- **Tone:** Generous, welcoming, festive, and heirloom-quality.
- **Visual Texture:** Warm parchment canvas, creamy ceramic surfaces, brushed brass, and fine metallic rule dividers evoking vintage culinary recipe volumes and heritage brand seals.
- **Atmosphere:** An inviting holiday kitchen filled with soft natural light, warm timber counters, a dusting of flour, rich pomegranate garnishes, and premium cookware.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen gastronomy with dramatic, celebratory accents:
- **Primary (`#9E1B32`)**: Pomegranate ruby red. Used for high-prestige editorial highlights, badge frames, contest phase markers, and hero subheadings.
- **Secondary (`#5E1A22`)**: Deep Tuscan wine. Reserved for primary interactive touchpoints, hero CTAs, and decisive interactive actions, giving them grounded weight and luxury tactile presence.
- **Tertiary (`#C9CCD1`)**: Subtle brushed metallic chrome. Reflects the stainless steel of pasta rollers and polished French kitchenware trims, deployed as delicate hairpins, badge rings, and divider lines.
- **Neutral (`#1D1B16`)**: Deep warm ink tone replacing harsh blacks. Used for typography and structural icons to maintain high contrast with an organic, letterpress-printed softness.
- **Surfaces (`#FBF6EE`, `#F6EFE5`, `#FFFFFF`)**: The canvas sits on cozy warm parchment (`#FBF6EE`). Secondary cards and tiered stages use warm biscuit cream (`#F6EFE5`), while elevated prize showcases and interactive recipes sit on pristine flour-white porcelain (`#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

The typography pairs the high-contrast classic elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clean, legible, humanistic geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Imparts heritage, gastronomic grandeur, and celebratory warmth. Used for stage titles, prize categories, recipes, and contest guidelines. It brings the prestige of vintage European magazines and traditional menu boards.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Ensures effortless readability for ingredient measurements, rules of entry, and submission forms.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Set in uppercase, tracked `label-sm` or `tag-editorial` to frame partner logos and culinary categories with artisanal precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous white space, organic breathing room, and a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a centered max width of 1240px:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer margins of 40px (`2.5rem`) to frame imagery and cards like printed plates.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Multi-column showcase cards compress gracefully into 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins. Stacked cards prioritize full-width touch affordances and immersive culinary photography.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are separated by `space-2xl` (64px) to retain an unhurried, editorial pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through soft, warm ceramic layering and tactile ambient shadows:
- **Canvas to Card Tiering:** The background sits on parchment `#FBF6EE`. Cards lift visually by using clean white porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) or light shortbread cream (`#F6EFE5`) surfaces outlined with subtle metallic borders (`1px solid rgba(201, 204, 209, 0.45)`).
- **Ambient Culinary Shadow:** Drop shadows are warm, low-opacity, and diffused—never harsh. Cards use a dual-stage shadow tinted with dark wine and warm sepia: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(94, 26, 34, 0.05), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(29, 27, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Raised & Hover States:** Interactive prize showcases and entry cards gain an elevated lift on hover (`0 16px 40px -6px rgba(94, 26, 34, 0.10)`) with a gentle 2px upward translation.
- **Metallic Dividers:** Section cutoffs and hero frames employ 1px hairline rules in `#C9CCD1` with gradient fadeouts to evoke artisanal metal embossing.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined **16px base radius** (`roundedness: 2`, `rounded-lg: 1rem`) across all primary presentation cards, recipe containers, and contest prize spotlights.

- **Primary Cards & Dialogue Sheets:** 16px (`1rem`) corner radius, offering soft architectural form reminiscent of glazed baking dishes and French ceramics.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Soft capsule shapes (`rounded-full` or 9999px) for contest phase tags, category chips, and hero call-to-actions, introducing an approachable, culinary smoothness.
- **Form Inputs & Search Fields:** 10px (`0.625rem`) for a tailored, premium utensil feel.
- **Embellishments:** Partner brand badges and award ribbons sit inside circular or softly octagonal geometric stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (CTA / Submit Recipe):** Deep wine background (`#5E1A22`), crisp ivory text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-full`, 16px vertical by 32px horizontal padding. Hover state deepens to `#461319` with a subtle warm glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(94, 26, 34, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Explore Prizes / Rules):** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in pomegranate red (`#9E1B32`), wine text (`#5E1A22`). Hover transitions to a soft parchment fill (`rgba(158, 27, 50, 0.06)`).
- **Tertiary / Link:** Warm ink (`#1D1B16`) with an animated fine underline and an optional right-chevron.

### Cards (Prizes, Recipe Submissions, Steps)
- **Prize Spotlight Cards:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) interior, 16px radius, bordered by a soft metallic hairline (`#C9CCD1`). Includes high-resolution imagery of Marcato Atlas machines and Emile Henry pie dishes, accompanied by Playfair Display headings and a delicate pomegranate tag.
- **Contest Step Cards:** Tinted in soft biscuit (`#F6EFE5`) with a 1px border. Feature an oversized Roman numeral or serif step number in `#9E1B32` with a short descriptive instruction below.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (e.g., "Active Stage", "Accepting Entries"):** Pill-shaped, 4px vertical / 12px horizontal padding, `#9E1B32` background with `#FFFFFF` text, styled in uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Cuisine Filters:** Parchment surface (`#FBF6EE`), 1px border (`#C9CCD1`), `#1D1B16` text. Active state turns solid wine (`#5E1A22`) with white text.

### Input Fields & Upload Drops
- **Form Inputs:** 48px height, rounded 10px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #C9CCD1`, placeholder text in muted ink (`rgba(29, 27, 22, 0.5)`). Focus rings glow with a gentle 2px pomegranate halo (`rgba(158, 27, 50, 0.2)`).
- **Recipe Photo Drag-and-Drop Area:** Dashed metallic border (`2px dashed #C9CCD1`), warm biscuit background (`#F6EFE5`), featuring a centered fork-and-spoon or camera icon and clear typographic guidance.

### Partner Seals & Contest Timeline
- **Collaboration Header:** Symmetrical arrangement placing the Russian culinary portal mark centrally between the Marcato and Emile Henry wordmarks, separated by fine vertical chrome dividers (`#C9CCD1`).
- **Timeline Milestone Track:** A 2px horizontal chrome guide punctuated by ruby circular nodal stamps denoting key dates (Submission Window, Jury Deliberation, Gala Winner Announcement).
